Fickle Foibles

Games I want to play.

Fickle Foibles is indie game development done in my spare time: one person, building the games I care about and would want to play myself. They take as long as they take.

Whatever the game, it should respect your time. Easy to pick up, easy to put down, and nothing lost when you do.

Dwindle Out now

A one-thumb solitaire for iPhone you can play in ninety seconds. Deal cards in a line and watch it dwindle. Free, with no ads, no accounts and nothing to buy.

Gemfall: The Dead City In development

A match-3 RPG built around a zombie apocalypse. Every scavenging run, fight and rescue is played on the board; between them, you hold a few city blocks and try to keep your people alive, fed and sane.

The Under-Warden In development

A turn-based roguelike: a retired specialist, a brass wand that talks back, and twenty-five floors of an underworld that is run like a filing office. Ten years in the making, and counting.
