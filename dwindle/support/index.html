How to play

Deal a card onto the end of the line. Look at any three cards in a row. If the two on the outside share a suit or a number, tap the one in the middle: it slides left onto its neighbour and the line closes up. A pile counts as the card on top. Stuck? Deal again. Changed your mind? Undo. When the deck runs out, count your piles. Two is a win; fewer is always better.

Common questions

Nothing is outlined and I can't tap anything.
No card can be collapsed right now. Tap the deck to deal the next card.
What does the play button do?
It hands the game to the computer, which plays at the speed shown on the pill beside it (½×, 2×, 4×). Pause hands the game back to you, with hints and undo. You can hide this button in Settings.
How do hints work?
On for your first three games, then off. Switch them on or off any time in Settings.
What is the Daily Deal?
The same shuffle for everyone that day. One attempt counts.
How do I reset my statistics?
Settings → Reset stats. This clears your statistics only; the app keeps working.
Where are the left-handed layout, dark theme, sound and haptics?
All in Settings.

Privacy

Dwindle collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics and no internet connection, and it asks for no permissions. See the privacy policy.
