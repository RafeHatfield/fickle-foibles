Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Dwindle is a solitaire game by Fickle Foibles for iPhone.

Dwindle does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

  • It has no user accounts and no sign-in.
  • It does not connect to the internet. It contains no analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting software of any kind.
  • It asks for no device permissions: no camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location or notifications.
  • Your game statistics and settings (games played, wins, best result, streaks, your preferences) are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are deleted when you delete the app. You can clear the statistics yourself from Settings → Reset stats.
  • The “Daily Deal” is computed from the date on your device; nothing is sent or received to provide it.

Because Dwindle collects nothing, there is nothing to access, correct, export or delete, and no data is processed for children or anyone else. Apple's App Store may collect information about your download under Apple's own privacy policy, which we do not control.

If this policy ever changes, the date above will change and the new text will appear at the same address before any new version of the app ships.
